What you will experience

Sharing and growing together

Couples yoga lets us practise poses together with another person, coordinating movement and breathing.

  1. Individual warm-up

  2. Poses in pairs

  3. Moving and breathing to the same rhythm

  4. Trust and care

After an individual warm-up session, we will experience collaboration, care, trust and good communication as we stretch, strengthen and improve the body's balance.

Always in a safe, comfortable space for everyone.

Who it is for

Bring whoever you like

  • Your partner
  • A friend
  • A relative or loved one
  • No experience needed

Invite your partner, a friend or any loved one and enjoy this experience outdoors, surrounded by nature, for a shared recharge of good energy.

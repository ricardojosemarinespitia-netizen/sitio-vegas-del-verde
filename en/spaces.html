Five venues to rent

Choose by size and by what is going to happen that day.

A venue for every plan

  • 100 People maximum 01 Alameda An open meadow with shade along the edges, for the day you do not want to end. Tap to see the uses See the uses for this venue

    Recommended uses for Alameda

    • Weddings

      The whole meadow takes a large ceremony, with shade along the edges for the guests.

    • Corporate

      It takes the most people: room for a product launch or a standing convention.

    • Birthdays

      Room to spare for the piñata, the long table and everyone running around.

    Get a quote for the Alameda

  • 150 People maximum 02 La Vega A flat expanse from end to end: the marquee, the dance floor and the sound system just go up. Tap to see the uses See the uses for this venue

    Recommended uses for La Vega

    • Weddings

      Not a single slope: the marquee and the dance floor go up level, with nothing to shim.

    • Birthdays

      Even ground from end to end: bouncy castles and games fit without hunting for a flat patch.

    • Wellness

      An even surface edge to edge: every mat sits at the same height.

    Get a quote for La Vega

  • 70 People maximum 03 Teatrino A clearing under the big tree where everyone fits in a single circle. The most intimate of the five. Tap to see the uses See the uses for this venue

    Recommended uses for Teatrino

    • Intimate weddings

      The tree canopy acts as the roof: small ceremonies, more sheltered than in the Alameda.

    • Birthdays

      The tree's shade covers almost the whole clearing: the party needs no separate canopy.

    • Talks and workshops

      The lawn works as a natural auditorium for a small group gathered in a circle.

    Get a quote for the Teatrino

  • 45 People maximum 04 Taller A cane roof and firm ground: here the rain watches from outside and the plan carries on. Tap to see the uses See the uses for this venue

    Recommended uses for Taller

    • Corporate

      It is the only roofed space on the property: the meeting goes ahead even if it rains.

    • Children's birthdays

      Firm, dry ground for the little ones' games, with no mud and no direct sun.

    • Workshops

      A whole class fits sitting down, with the materials under cover.

    Get a quote for the Taller

  • 30 Players and 20 spectators maximum 05 Cancha An open field edged and shaded by the bamboo grove: you come here to play until the light runs out. Tap to see the uses See the uses for this venue

    Recommended uses for Cancha

    • Sports birthdays

      The clear field is enough for a whole tournament or for the party games.

    • Company team-building

      A match in the middle changes any team day.

    • Family gatherings

      Afternoons of football and games, with the bamboo grove as edge and as shade.

    Get a quote for the Cancha

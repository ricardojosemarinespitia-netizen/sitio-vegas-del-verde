4. Venue rental conditions

  • The base rental is 4 hours, with the option of one additional hour.
  • It includes Rimax chairs and tables.
  • It does not include sound equipment; this is provided by the client.
  • Outside food and catering are allowed for events only.
  • The maximum capacities are: Alameda 100 people, La Vega 150 people, Teatrino 70 people, Taller 45 people, and Cancha 30 players plus 20 spectators. These may not be exceeded.

5. Opening hours and access

The property is open Monday to Sunday, from 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. Access may be restricted because of weather conditions, maintenance work or safety reasons. Vegas del Verde has private security, parking, restrooms and wifi.

6. Conduct and care for the surroundings

Vegas del Verde is a 4-hectare natural refuge with protected flora and fauna. Anyone visiting the property undertakes to respect the signage, not to feed or disturb the animals, not to remove plants, and to dispose of waste at the points provided for that purpose. Wildlife-watching activities always take place along the routes indicated by the staff.

7. Plant nursery

The nursery operates under ICA registration, Resolution no. 00000819 of 3 February 2025. The availability of species, fertilisers and decorative accessories depends on each season's production.

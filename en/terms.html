4. Venue Rental Conditions

  • The base rental is 4 hours, with the option of one additional hour.

  • Does not include a sound system; the client provides it.
  • Outside food and catering are allowed for events only.

The property is open Monday through Sunday, from 6:00 a. m. to 10:00 p. m. Access may be restricted due to weather conditions, maintenance work, or safety reasons. Vegas del Verde has private security, parking, restrooms, and wifi.

Vegas del Verde is a 4-hectare natural refuge with protected wildlife and plant life. Visitors agree to respect posted signage, to not feed or disturb the animals, to not remove plants, and to dispose of waste in the designated points. Birdwatching activities always take place along the routes indicated by staff.

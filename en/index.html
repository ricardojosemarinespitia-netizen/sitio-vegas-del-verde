About Us

A Natural Refuge

Four hectares of forest between two streams, just 10.2 km from Bucaramanga.

Vegas del Verde is a wooded 4-hectare property bordered by the Aranzoque stream and the La Florida creek.

Located in the heart of the Bucaramanga Metropolitan Area, it is a natural refuge that offers peace and safety amid the urban bustle.

Vereda Río Frío, Floridablanca · Santander, Colombia.

Birdwatching and Biodiversity

THE PROPERTY'S NATURAL WORLD

Four wooded hectares between the Aranzoque stream and the La Florida creek, in the heart of the Bucaramanga Metropolitan Area. Here, 101 bird species and 347 plant species have been recorded.

Guided Tour

SENDERO ECOVITAL

A guided tour for birdwatching and for observing the property's plants. The path is lined with guadua bamboo, Búcaro, and Caracolí trees, and follows the Aranzoque stream to where it meets the La Florida creek.

Birdwatching

101 Registered Bird Species

The property is a birdwatching destination within the Bucaramanga Metropolitan Area. Among the 101 registered species are one endemic to Colombia and seven boreal migrants.

Endemic to Colombia

Colombian Chachalaca

Ortalis columbiana

Endemic means it doesn't occur naturally anywhere else in the world. It's the most singular species in Vegas del Verde's bird record.

7

Boreal migratory species

Seven of the 101 registered species are boreal migrants: birds that travel down from the northern part of the continent and find refuge in the property's four hectares.

Schools and Educational Groups

LEARNING OUTDOORS

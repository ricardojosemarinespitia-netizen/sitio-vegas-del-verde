  • 01 Weddings and concerts

    The party starts when the bulbs come on

    The ceremony under the tree, the marquee strung with bulbs and the music until the sky turns blue.

    Set up in the Alameda or in La Vega.

    Try a different kind of plan: wedding or concert

  • 02 Birthdays and family

    Blow out the candles under a tree

    Picnic tables, garlands between the branches and room to spare for running around.

    Fits in La Vega and in the Teatrino.

    Try a different kind of plan: birthday or family gathering

  • 03 Companies

    Get your team out of the boardroom

    A translucent marquee on the lawn, tall tables for coffee and a ring of armchairs.

    Works well in the Alameda and in the Taller.

    Try a different kind of plan: corporate meeting or company event

  • 04 Wellness

    Breathe deep — there is air to spare here

    • Therapeutic walks
    • Functional training
    • Fitness conditioning sessions
    • Pilates
    • Yoga
    • Mindfulness
    • Workshop on managing emotions

    And an open-air gym, for anyone who would rather break a sweat.

    Held in the Teatrino or on the Cancha.

    Try a different kind of plan: wellness activity

    A workshop where the table is outdoors

    • Educational workshops
    • Gardening workshops
    • Open-air painting workshops

    Bring the group and the subject; the forest brings the light and the model.

    Held in the Taller and in the Teatrino.

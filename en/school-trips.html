Vegas del Verde · Floridablanca, Santander

Five living classrooms

All included in the pass

Each visit goes through them in whatever order the teacher decides.

  • Plant nursery

    Growing native plants and propagating them in a nursery that really works.

  • Ecovital Trail

    An ecological walk through the forest for environmental learning.

  • Pollinator Garden

    Watching birds and butterflies in their natural habitat.

  • Covered terrace

    An equipped room for workshops, talks and audiovisual screenings.

  • Football pitch

    A sports area for recreational activities and football training for beginners.

The school brings the teaching plan and the teacher.

Vegas del Verde does not provide teaching support or binoculars.

Community service

Service hours in the forest

Four options so your students can complete their school community service hours (servicio social) in the forest.

  • Birdwatching workshop

    Observing and identifying local species.

  • Nursery guardians

    Caring for and maintaining the plant nursery.

  • Pollinator guardians

    Protecting pollinators and their habitats.

  • Junior butterfly scientists

    Researching and recording the butterfly life cycle.

What it is and why here

One day to count the birds of the world

October Big Day is eBird’s global birdwatching day, run by the Cornell Lab of Ornithology. People all over the planet head outdoors to watch birds and record what they see. Vegas del Verde takes part as an observation site: an urban forest cared for as a reserve.

Before you come

Frequently asked questions

Is there an entry fee?

No. Entry is free. You only pay if you order breakfast or snacks.

If entry is free, why book?

To save your spot and, if you like, your breakfast. It is done on WhatsApp.

Do I need to know about birds?

No. Everyone is welcome and it is an event for the whole family.

Do I have to stay all morning?

The day runs from 6:30 a.m. to 12 noon. You can come at any time within those hours.

What should I wear?

Long trousers, a long-sleeved shirt and tall boots. Bring water and insect repellent, plus binoculars and a notebook if you have them.
